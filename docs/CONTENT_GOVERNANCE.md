# Public content governance

This guide applies to public-facing JIIT Youth Club website content and the administrative workflow that publishes it.

## Content ownership
- Publish only club, event, team, and contact information confirmed by an authorized JYC editor.
- Treat dates, venues, registration links, and contact channels as time-sensitive; verify them before publishing.
- Obtain appropriate permission before publishing identifiable student photographs, quotes, or personal contact details.
- Keep draft content private until it has been reviewed and explicitly published.

## Editorial checks
- Use consistent club and event names.
- Include a clear title, date, venue (when confirmed), and useful action for event listings.
- Provide meaningful alternative text for informative images; use empty alt text for purely decorative images.
- Check links and mobile layouts before publishing.
- Remove expired registration calls to action and mark postponed or cancelled events clearly.

## Operational checks
- Confirm the editor is authorized before changing public content.
- Verify public visibility after publishing and confirm drafts remain unpublished.
- Do not put secrets or privileged credentials in client-side content or configuration.
- Keep a correction path available for outdated or inaccurate information.

This document describes editorial practice; it does not replace the application's authorization or database policies.
